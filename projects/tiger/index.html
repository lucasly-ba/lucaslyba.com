Overview

The Tiger Compiler is the flagship project of EPITA's compiler course: a complete compiler for the Tiger language, built in modern C++ to teach compiler construction and software-design patterns. Before anything else, I built the whole thing myself as a student — lexer, parser, binding, type-checking, escape analysis, IR translation and a back-end — following the full assignment chain front-end to back-end.

I was then accepted into the assistant team as a Tiger maintainer working on SSA. SSA optimization has been passed down through generations of maintainers: I am the 7th maintainer of the 7th generation to work on it — and the one who finally got it merged. I built on the merged student work handed down from Elsa Keirouz, and Julie Fiadino helped me a lot throughout. The optimizer was written the hard way — reading the algorithms in Andrew W. Appel's Modern Compiler Implementation in ML, thinking them through, and implementing them by hand.

Five SSA optimization passes

Each pass runs on the Low-Level IR (LIR) after conversion to SSA form. Below: a small program, its LIR before the pass, and the same LIR after.

01 Constant folding--constant-folding --ssa

Expressions over known constants (3 + 4, 10 * 2) are evaluated at compile time instead of at runtime.

Before — LIR
After — folded

02 Constant propagation--constant-propagation --ssa

A variable known to hold a constant is substituted directly at its uses, so the value is never reloaded from memory.

Before — LIR
After — propagated

03 Copy propagation--copy-propagation --ssa

When one variable is just a copy of another (e := d), every use of the copy is replaced by the original and the copy is eliminated.

Before — LIR
After — copy removed

04 Dead-code elimination--dead-code-elimination --ssa

An assignment whose result is never read (unused := 999) is removed entirely — no computation, no memory write.

Before — LIR
After — dead store gone

05 Unreachable-code elimination--constant-conditions --ssa

A branch with a constant condition (if 1 = 1) is resolved at compile time and the dead branch is dropped.

Before — LIR
After — branch removed

All passes at once

Chained together (--ssa-optimize), what looks like six variables and a branch collapses to a single constant. The compiler sees print_int(82).
