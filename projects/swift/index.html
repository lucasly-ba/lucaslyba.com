Overview

I contribute to the Swift toolchain — one of the largest open-source compilers in production. This means navigating a massive C++ codebase, opening pull requests, and working through the upstream review process with the Swift community.

Why it matters

Contributing to a compiler of this scale is a masterclass in reading unfamiliar code, isolating issues across a huge pipeline, and writing changes that meet a high review bar. It pairs directly with my work building Swift from source on NixOS.
