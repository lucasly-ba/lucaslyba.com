Overview

Clearing snow from a city is a routing problem: every street must be covered by a fleet at minimum time and cost. Urban Snow Route Optimizer models the city as a graph from real OpenStreetMap data and computes efficient routes for both snowplows — which must traverse every street, an arc-routing problem — and drones, which survey from above.

What it does

  • Builds street-network graphs from OSM via OSMnx
  • Solves coverage routing for snowplow fleets across neighborhoods
  • Plans complementary drone survey routes
  • Renders animated route playback and interactive Folium maps
