Overview

Memex indexes your conversations from ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity and turns them into one searchable knowledge base — auto-tagging content, grouping related threads, and syncing across devices. I founded it and built the entire product stack end-to-end.

What I built

  • Manifest V3 browser extension in TypeScript & Vite
  • Search and conversation indexing infrastructure
  • Cross-device synchronization
  • Thread grouping & knowledge organization
  • Billing, subscriptions, authentication & accounts
